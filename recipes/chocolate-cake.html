Chocolate Cake

Description

Chocolate cake is a delicious dessert made with cocoa powder, flour, sugar, eggs, and other baking ingredients. It has a soft, moist texture and a rich chocolate flavor. It can be topped with chocolate frosting and served at birthdays, celebrations, or whenever you want a sweet treat.

Ingredients

  • 1 and 1/2 cups all-purpose flour
  • 1 cup sugar
  • 1/3 cup unsweetened cocoa powder
  • 1 teaspoon baking powder
  • 1 teaspoon baking soda
  • 1/4 teaspoon salt
  • 2 eggs
  • 1/2 cup milk
  • 1/4 cup vegetable oil
  • 1 teaspoon vanilla extract
  • 1/2 cup hot water
  • Chocolate frosting (optional)

Steps

  1. Preheat the oven to 180°C (350°F).
  2. Grease a cake pan and lightly dust it with flour.
  3. In a bowl, mix the flour, sugar, cocoa powder, baking powder, baking soda, and salt.
  4. Add the eggs, milk, oil, and vanilla extract. Mix until the batter is smooth.
  5. Carefully stir in the hot water. The batter will be thin.
  6. Pour the batter into the prepared cake pan.
  7. Bake for 30–35 minutes, or until a toothpick inserted into the center comes out clean.
  8. Allow the cake to cool completely before removing it from the pan.
  9. Spread chocolate frosting on top if desired.
  10. Slice and serve.
