Lasagna Recipe

Description

Lasagna is a classic Italian dish made with layers of pasta, rich tomato sauce, seasoned meat, and melted cheese.

Ingredients

  • 9 lasagna sheets
  • 500 g ground beef
  • 1 medium onion, finely chopped
  • 2–3 cloves garlic, minced
  • 400 g tomato sauce
  • 2 tablespoons tomato paste
  • 2 tablespoons olive oil
  • 1 teaspoon dried oregano
  • 1 teaspoon dried basil
  • 2 cups shredded mozzarella cheese
  • ½ cup grated Parmesan cheese
  • 2 cups béchamel (white) sauce

Steps

  1. Preheat the oven to 180°C (350°F).
  2. Heat olive oil in a pan and sauté the chopped onion and garlic until soft.
  3. Add the ground beef and cook until browned.
  4. Stir in the tomato sauce, tomato paste, oregano, basil, salt, and black pepper. Simmer for 15–20 minutes.
  5. Prepare the lasagna sheets according to the package instructions, if required.
  6. Spread a layer of meat sauce in a baking dish, then add lasagna sheets, béchamel sauce, and mozzarella cheese.
  7. Repeat the layers until all the ingredients are used, finishing with béchamel sauce, mozzarella, and Parmesan cheese.
  8. Bake for 30–40 minutes, until the cheese is golden and bubbling.
  9. Remove from the oven and let the lasagna rest for 10–15 minutes before slicing.
  10. Serve warm and enjoy your homemade lasagna!
