Chicken Biryani

Description

Chicken biryani is a popular Indian rice dish made with fragrant basmati rice, tender chicken, and aromatic spices. The ingredients are cooked together to create a flavorful and delicious meal that is enjoyed on special occasions and as a satisfying everyday dish.

Ingredients

  • 500 g chicken
  • 2 cups basmati rice
  • 2 onions, thinly sliced
  • 2 tomatoes, chopped
  • 1/2 cup plain yogurt
  • 2 tablespoons ginger-garlic paste
  • 2 tablespoons cooking oil or ghee
  • 1 teaspoon red chili powder
  • 1/2 teaspoon turmeric powder
  • 1 teaspoon biryani masala
  • Mint and coriander leaves
  • Salt to taste
  • Whole spices such as cinnamon, cloves, and cardamom

Steps

  1. Wash and soak the basmati rice for about 20 minutes.
  2. Marinate the chicken with yogurt, ginger-garlic paste, chili powder, turmeric, biryani masala, and salt.
  3. Heat oil or ghee in a pan and fry the sliced onions until golden brown.
  4. Add the tomatoes and marinated chicken. Cook until the chicken is nearly done.
  5. Boil the rice separately with salt and whole spices until it is about 70 percent cooked, then drain it.
  6. Layer the partially cooked rice over the chicken. Add mint and coriander leaves.
  7. Cover and cook on low heat for 15–20 minutes, until the rice and chicken are fully cooked.
  8. Gently mix the layers and serve hot.
